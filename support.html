Tragent support

Help with your account, connected inbox, subscription, notifications or anything else in Tragent.

Contact support

Email support@trytragent.com. Include a short description of the problem and the app version shown on your device. Do not email passwords, verification codes, OAuth tokens, or customer message content.

Connected inbox

You can connect, reconnect or disconnect Gmail and Microsoft Outlook from Settings in the app. If mail has stopped updating, open Connected accounts and follow the reconnect prompt.

Subscriptions

Use Restore Purchases on the subscription screen if an existing App Store purchase is not recognised. You can manage or cancel your subscription through your Apple Account subscription settings.

Account deletion

You can permanently delete your Tragent account from Settings. Deleting the Tragent account does not cancel an App Store subscription, so manage that separately through Apple if necessary.
