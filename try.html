FAQs

About the
live demo

What happens when I send a test enquiry?

Tragent processes the message as if it had arrived from a real customer. You’ll see it recognise the enquiry, create the job and generate a professional reply step by step.

Is the reply generated live?

Yes. It is generated from the details you enter, using the same reply pipeline that handles real customer enquiries. It is not a recording or a pre-written response.

Will the demo connect to or read my inbox?

No. The live demo only uses the information you enter into this form. It does not connect to Gmail or Outlook, and it cannot read any other messages.

Will you send the reply to my inbox?

No. The demo is completely self-contained. The generated reply appears here on the page so you can see exactly what your customer would receive without checking your inbox.

How quickly should the reply arrive?

Most test replies appear on the page in under 60 seconds. The exact timing depends on the live reply-generation service.

Can I try it more than once?

Yes. You can send another enquiry to test a different type of job or wording. Reasonable usage limits may apply to keep the demo available for everyone.
