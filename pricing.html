Frequently asked
questions

How much does Tragent cost?

Tragent costs £29.99 a month. Choose yearly billing to save 20%, paying £287.90 for the year. Nothing is charged until your free trial ends.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. New subscribers can start with a seven-day free trial. The app shows the renewal price and billing period before you confirm.

Do monthly and yearly plans include different features?

No. Both billing options unlock the same Tragent features. The difference is simply how often you pay.

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Yes. You can manage or cancel your subscription through your Apple account. Access continues until the end of the current billing period.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your selected subscription renews through Apple unless you cancel before the trial finishes. The app shows the exact price and renewal details before you begin.

Can I see Tragent before subscribing?

Yes. Use the live demo to see how Tragent responds to a sample enquiry before downloading the app.
