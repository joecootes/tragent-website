Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Tragent collects, how we use it, how we protect it, and your rights.

Tragent helps trade businesses automatically respond to customer enquiries, manage jobs and follow up with customers.

Who we are

Tragent is operated from the United Kingdom.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your privacy rights, contact us at hello@trytragent.com.

Information we collect

When you use Tragent we may collect:

  • Your phone number
  • Your business profile information
  • Your app settings
  • Your connected email account information
  • Customer and job information created from enquiries
  • Messages and attachments required to provide the service
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information

If you use Try It Live on our website we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your enquiry text
  • Whether you wish to join the beta or receive updates

This information is used only to provide the requested demonstration, prevent abuse and contact you if requested.

Connected email accounts

Tragent allows you to connect supported email providers, including Gmail and Microsoft Outlook.

Once you authorise access, Tragent may access information including:

  • Sender and recipient email addresses
  • Subject lines
  • Email message content
  • Attachments where required
  • Email thread information
  • Message metadata necessary to provide the service

This information is used only to:

  • Detect genuine customer enquiries
  • Create and update job records
  • Detect customer replies
  • Generate AI-assisted responses
  • Send automatic acknowledgement emails
  • Send follow-up emails
  • Keep your job timeline up to date
  • Provide the Tragent features you enable

Tragent stores the authentication tokens required to keep your email account connected.

These credentials remain stored until you disconnect the email account, revoke access through your email provider or delete your Tragent account.

Tragent never accesses your email account until you explicitly authorise access through the relevant provider's authentication process.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Connect your email account
  • Detect customer enquiries
  • Create and manage jobs
  • Generate AI-assisted responses
  • Automatically send replies and follow-ups according to your Tragent settings
  • Notify you about customer activity
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve reliability and fix bugs

We do not use your information for advertising.

AI processing

Tragent uses trusted AI service providers to:

  • Understand customer enquiries
  • Extract job information
  • Generate responses
  • Generate follow-up messages

Only the information necessary to provide these features is processed.

Google API data is not used to develop, improve or train general-purpose or shared artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Tragent does not permit third-party AI providers to use Google API data to train their own general-purpose AI or machine learning models.

Sharing and processors

To operate Tragent we use trusted service providers that provide services including:

  • Cloud hosting
  • Authentication
  • Email provider integrations
  • AI processing
  • Crash reporting
  • Email delivery
  • Messaging infrastructure

These providers process information only as necessary to operate Tragent and are not permitted to use your information for their own purposes.

Tragent does not:

  • Sell your personal information
  • Sell Google API data
  • Share Google API data with advertisers
  • Use Google API data for targeted advertising
  • Transfer Google API data to data brokers

Crash and diagnostic data

We use Sentry to collect:

  • Crash reports
  • Diagnostic logs
  • Device information
  • App version
  • Performance information

This information helps us identify bugs, improve reliability and maintain the security of Tragent.

Data protection

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect your information.

These measures include:

  • Encrypted network connections
  • Secure storage of authentication credentials
  • Access controls
  • Production system protections
  • Monitoring and logging

While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data retention and deletion

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide Tragent, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and maintain the security of the service.

Disconnecting an email account removes the stored authentication credentials and prevents Tragent from accessing that account in the future.

Deleting your Tragent account removes your:

  • Account
  • Profile
  • Authentication credentials
  • Connected email accounts
  • Jobs
  • Automation settings
  • Associated customer information

from active Tragent systems where reasonably practical.

Limited information may temporarily remain in secure backups until those backups are overwritten or expire.

Users may also revoke Tragent's access directly from their email provider's account settings.

Your rights

If you are located in the United Kingdom or European Economic Area you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion
  • Restrict processing
  • Receive a copy of your personal information

To exercise these rights, contact hello@trytragent.com.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Tragent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Information received from Google APIs is used only to provide and improve Tragent's user-facing features.

Google API data is not:

  • Sold
  • Used for advertising or marketing
  • Shared with data brokers
  • Used for creditworthiness decisions
  • Used to develop, improve or train general-purpose artificial intelligence or machine learning models
  • Transferred to third parties except where necessary to provide the services requested by the user

Replying, following up, and winning more jobs for trade businesses.
