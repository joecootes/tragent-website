FAQs

Common
questions

Which inboxes does Tragent work with?

Gmail is available today, with more inbox connections being rolled out. If your business uses another provider, get in touch and we’ll tell you what’s available.

What does the automatic reply say?

A short, professional response in your business name. It acknowledges the enquiry and asks for useful missing details, such as a postcode, photos or a contact number.

Will it reply to spam or newsletters?

No. Marketing and automated messages are filtered out before a reply is considered. If a message is unclear, Tragent leaves it for you to review.

Can I turn automation off?

Yes. You can pause follow-ups for any job, or turn automatic replies off entirely while still using Tragent to organise enquiries and jobs.

What happens if I disconnect my inbox?

Tragent immediately stops watching that inbox and won’t send anything from it. Your existing jobs and conversations stay in the app.
